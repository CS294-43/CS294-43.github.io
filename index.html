Large Vision, Language, and Action Models

CS 294-43 explores the research frontier at the intersection of visual perception, language understanding, embodied intelligence, and action.

Tuesdays · 6:00–8:00 PM Gateway B1019 Instructor: Trevor Darrell

About the course

Recent advances in foundation models are bringing perception, reasoning, and interaction closer together. This graduate seminar examines the methods, systems, and research questions behind large-scale vision, language, and action models.

Topics may include vision representation learning, multimodal reasoning, visual agents, image and video generation, robotics, world models, and learning from interaction. The final set of topics, papers, and assignments will be announced by the course staff.

For reference, see the Fall 2025 course website and Fall 2024 course website.
